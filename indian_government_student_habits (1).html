Build Good Habits, Build a Better Future 🌟

Good habits help students become healthier, more responsible, confident and successful. Small positive actions every day can make a big difference in life.

✅ Good Habits

📚 Study Regularly

  • Complete homework on time
  • Read every day
  • Ask questions when confused
  • Keep schoolwork organized

🥗 Eat Healthy

  • Eat fruits and vegetables
  • Drink enough water
  • Have regular meals
  • Choose nutritious foods

🧹 Stay Clean

  • Wash hands regularly
  • Keep your classroom clean
  • Maintain personal hygiene
  • Keep books and bags organized

🏃 Stay Active

  • Play outdoor games
  • Take regular movement breaks
  • Spend time outdoors
  • Maintain a healthy routine

❌ Habits to Avoid

📱 Excessive Screen Time

  • Avoid unnecessary phone use
  • Take breaks from screens
  • Don't let screens replace sleep or study

🍔 Unhealthy Eating

  • Avoid too much junk food
  • Don't skip regular meals
  • Choose healthier alternatives

😴 Poor Sleep Routine

  • Avoid staying up unnecessarily late
  • Keep a regular sleep schedule
  • Give your body enough rest

📖 Procrastination

  • Don't keep homework until the last minute
  • Make a simple daily plan
  • Break large tasks into smaller tasks

🌟 Student Success Tips

⭐ Set small goals every day.

⭐ Be respectful to teachers, parents and classmates.

⭐ Keep your study area organized.

⭐ Learn from mistakes instead of giving up.

⭐ Balance study, exercise, hobbies and rest.

⭐ Help others whenever you can.
